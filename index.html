I'm Pranay Sumit Singh, a BSc Information Technology student with a passion for technology, creative design, and digital innovation. I enjoy exploring new ideas, experimenting with modern tools, and turning imagination into real-world projects.

From building websites and creating event designs to exploring web development and AI-powered creative tools, I like combining technical thinking with creativity to make meaningful digital experiences.
